    How grading works

    Accuracy is AP@0.5, or the frame-level ROC AUC: how well a frame's top confidence separates frames with an object from frames without, which is Blue Iris's alert decision; against labels, or against a reference model where a dataset has none. Speed is the full request p50. Every device is checked to detect the same objects as the CPU. The recommended device of a model is its best-graded device whose detections agree with the CPU; within 5% of the best speed the configured device is kept. The same predictions are scored at every confidence (no extra inference) to find each model's best confidence on each device for your goal. Speeds are per image, one at a time; the service pipelines decode, preprocessing and inference, so its throughput is higher than 1000 / latency implies. The benchmark CLI writes the same results file.
