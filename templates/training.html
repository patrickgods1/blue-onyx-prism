Import footage

Frames from your own videos and image folders (Blue Iris clip and alert folders included), a video URL, or pinned open footage go into the dataset folder {{ dataset_dir }} and the Review queue, like collected frames. Scene changes are kept at a bounded rate (FFmpeg select), near duplicates dropped (dHash), and the frames picked by SigLIP 2 k-center, balanced by day / night / IR. Every frame records its source and the licence you confirm. Details: docs/TRAINING.md.

Sites that list unsecured or private cameras (Insecam and the like) are refused, and live streams are never read. Blue Iris .bvr clips cannot be read: export them to MP4 in Blue Iris first.

Auto-label

Proposes boxes for dataset frames as pending auto boxes on the Review page: MM-Grounding-DINO (open vocabulary, the class list plus training.synonyms) and RF-DETR L (COCO classes) fused, person and single-labeler boxes checked by Qwen3-VL, animals ranked by SigLIP 2. A box is marked accept only when both labelers agree at 0.6 or more and no check objects; night persons and animals are always review. Nothing is accepted or deleted for you. Needs an NVIDIA GPU or Apple silicon; re-labelling with a trained model (self-training) runs anywhere.

Frames: key=value pairs, comma-separated: bucket, scene (day, night, ir), camera, label (a class), status (unreviewed, reviewed, skipped, any), source (queue, all; default all here, while the Review page's button uses its own filters), auto (review, accept, nothing, none: never auto-labelled), import (an import id, or any), again=1 (also frames these labelers already looked at; skipped otherwise), limit. A re-label looks at every frame by default and sends the ones whose reviewed labels the model disagrees with back to review, first in the queue.
