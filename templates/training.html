Training

Fine-tune a detection model on your own frames: import footage, let the auto-labelers propose boxes, review and export them on the Review page, train here, then use the model. Every trained model is exported to ONNX, added to the config and benchmarked on held-out frames from cameras and days it did not train on. Details and licences: docs/TRAINING.md.

Import footage

Frames from your own videos and image folders, a video URL or pinned open footage go into the dataset folder ({{ dataset_dir }}) and the Review queue, like collected frames. Scene changes are kept at a bounded rate, near duplicates dropped, and the frames picked by SigLIP 2, balanced by day / night / IR. Every frame records its source and licence.

Source

Blue Iris clip and alert folders work. .bvr clips cannot be read: export them to MP4 in Blue Iris first.

Downloaded with yt-dlp. Sites that list unsecured or private cameras (Insecam and the like) are refused, and live streams are never read.

Its licence is recorded with every frame; no confirmation needed.

Stored with every frame.

dHash: image folders import without a GPU or a download.

Scene changes kept at most at this rate.

Near duplicates (dHash) within this time are dropped.

Auto-label

Proposes boxes for dataset frames as pending auto boxes on the Review page. Nothing is accepted or deleted for you: a box is marked accept only when both labelers agree at 0.6 or more and no check objects; night persons and animals are always review.

Labelers

Open vocabulary: the class list plus training.synonyms.

Checks person and single-labeler boxes.

Frames whose reviewed labels it disagrees with go back to review, first in the queue.

Which frames

Default: unreviewed; a re-label: any.

Most frames, in queue order.

Other filters

key=value pairs, comma-separated: bucket, import (an import id, or any), source (queue, all; default all here), and any key above (scene day, night, ir; camera; label; status unreviewed, reviewed, skipped, any; auto review, accept, nothing, none; again=1; limit). A key given here wins over its field.
