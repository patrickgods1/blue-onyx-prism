Import from X-AnyLabeling

Reads the .json files of an exported xanylabeling folder after you edited them in X-AnyLabeling. A file counts once its image is checked there; its rectangles become the frame's boxes (boxes you deleted are kept as rejected).

Classes applies now

The class list of the Review tab and of the exports (collect.classes); YOLO class ids follow this order, so append rather than reorder once you exported. Ticking hide on a class you don't want hides it from Review (pickers, shortcuts and its boxes) and rejects its pending boxes on the unreviewed frames right away, as removing a class does; reviewed frames with accepted boxes of it change only if you say so when asked. The class keeps its place and YOLO id. The auto-label defaults follow the classes the enabled models detect (Config page).
