Check the dataset

Short throwaway models each learn from most of your reviewed camera days, then look at the days they never saw and say where they disagree with your labels: a box nobody drew, a label no model found, an object that looks like another class. The check changes no box. It only leaves suggestions on the frames for you to confirm on the Review tab. With 4 folds of 8 epochs it takes about an hour on a mid-range GPU, and the GPU is busy meanwhile: other jobs wait their turn.

Each fold model trains on the other folds' camera days. More folds train longer for a little more training data.

A scene or camera also narrows what the fold models train on.
