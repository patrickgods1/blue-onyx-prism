Check the dataset

Short throwaway models each learn from most of your reviewed camera days, then look at the days they never saw and say where they disagree with your labels: a box nobody drew, a label no model found, an object that looks like another class. The check changes no box. It only leaves suggestions on the frames for you to confirm on the Review tab. With 4 folds of 8 epochs it takes about an hour on a mid-range GPU, and the GPU is busy meanwhile: other jobs wait their turn.

Each fold model trains on the other folds' camera days. More folds train longer for a little more training data.

A scene or camera also narrows what the fold models train on.

Static objects

A static spot is a place where the same parked object (a car in the driveway, a boat on its trailer) is labelled in most frames of one camera. A frame that shows nothing else there teaches the model little, so a training run keeps only a set number of such frames per spot (20 by default) and leaves the rest out. The same places are where the dataset check looks hardest for a box nobody drew.

Too small

Objects smaller than about one patch of the model's input cannot be learned. With a size floor they are left out of the labels at export (your labels are not changed), and a detection on one counts neither for nor against a model on held-out frames.
