    How review works

    Frames collected from your cameras (Collect tab), one at a time, most uncertain first. Decide every box: accept the real objects, reject the false ones (kept as hard negatives), restore a wrongly suppressed box, draw what the model missed. They stay on this machine in {{ dir }}. Format: docs/DATASET.md. After an auto-label run, boxes marked auto: accept are accepted on save and auto: review ones (marked ?) ask first; frames with only accept boxes can be accepted in bulk. Press ? for the keys.

    An auto-label run also sorts its new boxes, by what its test runs measured. Likely boxes (★, a score of 0.25 or more and a VLM yes; 77% real on public sets, 96% on a fixed camera) come first, in the queue and in the box list, and c accepts them all. The other flagged boxes are drawn quieter; Shift+R clears them. Boxes under 0.15 (5% real on public sets) are hidden: no negatives, nothing lost. Show N hidden or i brings them back dimmed, and accepting one makes it a label. A VLM no never hides a box, trucks and SAM 3 boxes are never hidden, and a frame with hidden boxes needs a reviewer: no bulk save takes it. About 2% of the boxes that would be hidden stay visible (audit sample: normally hidden); your verdicts on them are the Hidden-box check in the counts.

    Fixed cameras: the run remembers what you decided about a place. A box at a spot you rejected on at least two frames in the last 30 days is hidden too (you rejected this spot N times), unless it scores 0.5 or more, is likely, or a box was ever accepted there; about 2% stay visible as a check. Real, parked (t) accepts a real object that stays put and remembers its spot: later runs accept the same class there, unless the VLM says no, so a car that left is not labelled. Those frames stay unreviewed. When the camera's view changes, use Camera moved: forget spots in Panel options.

    Labelling rules:

    • A tight box around the visible part of the object.
    • Parked cars are cars: an object left unlabelled teaches the model it is background.
    • A cyclist is a person box plus a bicycle box.
    • A person inside a vehicle only if clearly visible.
    • An object cut by the frame edge only if you can still recognise it.
    • Dark frames: look at them brightened (b) before deciding. A real object you still cannot name for sure is unsure (d): kept, left out of YOLO training, ignored by the benchmark set. Reject what is not an object at all.
    • Why a box was wrong is optional: after r, Shift+1-9 (insect / web, IR glare, reflection, ...) marks it for hard-negative mining.
    • Before saving, look over the whole frame for objects the model missed: they are the commonest label error.
