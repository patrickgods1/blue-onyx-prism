Import footage

Frames from your own videos and image folders, a video URL or pinned open footage go into the dataset folder ({{ dataset_dir }}) and the Review queue, like collected frames. Scene changes are kept at a bounded rate, near duplicates dropped, and the frames picked by SigLIP 2, balanced by day / night / IR. Every frame records its source and licence.

Source

Blue Iris clip and alert folders work. .bvr clips cannot be read: export them to MP4 in Blue Iris first.

Downloaded with yt-dlp. Sites that list unsecured or private cameras (Insecam and the like) are refused, and live streams are never read.

Its licence is recorded with every frame; no confirmation needed.

Stored with every frame.

Empty = the dataset folder, shown on the Collect and Review tabs. Another folder gets the same layout (<scene>/<camera>/<date>/, sidecars, review queue); review them by choosing this folder on the Review tab.

Sampling options

dHash: image folders import without a GPU or a download.

0.01 = motion in a fixed camera, 0.3 = cuts (FFmpeg select).

At most this many frames read from one video; the 20,000 of an import are shared fairly by cameras, videos and images.
